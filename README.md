## UTOPAY — payment gateway for cards and crypto

[UTOPAY](https://utopay.app) lets online businesses accept **cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Revolut** and **crypto (USDT, ETH, BNB and more)** and get paid straight to their own wallet. Built for high-risk and international merchants.

- 💳 [Card gateway](https://utopay.app/gateway) — 4.5% all-in, payouts in USDC
- 🪙 [Crypto gateway](https://utopay.app/crypto) — 1.5%, forwarded on-chain to your wallet · [Accept USDT](https://utopay.app/crypto/usdt)
- 🔗 [Payment links](https://utopay.app/payment-links) — get paid without a website
- 🧩 [CMS plugins](https://utopay.app/plugins) — WooCommerce, WHMCS, OpenCart, PrestaShop, MODX, EDD, Paid Memberships Pro, Tilda
- 📚 [API documentation](https://utopay.app/docs) · [Payment gateway API](https://utopay.app/payment-gateway-api)

**Repositories:** [utopay-sdk](https://github.com/UtoPayApp/utopay-sdk) — official API clients for Node.js, PHP and Python, OpenAPI spec and examples.
